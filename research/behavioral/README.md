# Behavioral finance: 0DTE lottery premium



According to prospect theory, people overweight small probabilities — that is why

they overpay for lottery tickets. We asked whether the same happens in far-OTM 0DTE

SPX options, which are lottery tickets by construction: tiny chance of finishing ITM,

big payout if they do. Measured over 1,068 trading days (2022–2026), snapshot at

13:00, settled against the 16:00 close.



\*\*Puts:\*\* confirmed, and monotone in OTM distance. Seller margin rises from 24% to

49% to 87% per premium dollar across the 0.5–1% / 1–2% / 2–5% OTM buckets; buyers

in the far bucket pay up to 8x the fair ITM probability.



\*\*Calls: no conclusion possible.\*\* A single day (2025-04-09, +9.5%) produced over

95% of all call payouts — effectively one observation.



\*\*The seller's distribution is the warning.\*\* The 2–5% OTM put seller wins on 99.81%

of days (median day: keep 100% of premium) — it looks like free money. But skew is

\-32.7, and one afternoon (2024-12-18, Fed decision) cost -147.88 per premium dollar

collected. Win rate tells you how often you win; the worst day decides whether you

survive. And our sample contains no 1987-style crash — the really bad day is not in

the data yet.



\*\*Caveats:\*\* mid prices (a real seller gets the bid), no fees, no margin. This is a

measurement of the premium, not a strategy.
